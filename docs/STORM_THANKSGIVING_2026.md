## 1. What this means for Doylestown and Newtown

You are inland, so the story here is **wind first, rain second**. The coastal and tidal flooding (the headline at the Jersey Shore) does not hit you directly.

A strong coastal low forms off the Southeast coast Wednesday, deepens fast and runs north. It is **all rain** for Bucks County, no wintry mix. Rain starts around dawn Thursday. **Gusts build through the morning and hold near 50 to 55 mph from about 10 AM to 4 PM**, then ease through the evening. Rain is steady all day and heaviest from about 1 PM Thursday to early Friday. It eases Friday, with a second round of gusty southwest wind (gusts 40 to 50 mph possible) Friday afternoon.

What it means locally (from the NWS expectations for inland areas):
- NWS says widespread tree damage and power outages are possible across much of the area for this storm, most likely toward the coast, so expect some downed limbs and scattered outages in Bucks.
- Ponding and urban flooding in poor-drainage spots, and watch the creeks (Neshaminy, Core Creek and similar). It has been dry and the storm is fast, so NWS expects flooding to stay confined, and WPC has only a Marginal risk.
- The tidal Delaware River (the river towns in lower Bucks) may see minor flooding starting with Thursday afternoon's high tide and lasting into Friday. That does not apply to Doylestown or Newtown themselves.
- Travel: bad Thursday morning to evening for high-profile vehicles, with gusty bridges, and visibility and ponding on roads. If you head to the shore, tidal flooding there is major.
